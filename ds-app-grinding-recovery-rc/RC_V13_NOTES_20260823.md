## IQC Honeywell OCR stabilization
Field evidence before v13:
- local multi-photo OCR showed alternating behavior around 3/6 success on the same fixed 3-photo set.
- v12 could regress RT 112639 from 13/13 to 12/13 and RT 113374 to about 5/19.

Design decision:
- no parallel OCR workers.
- one-time session warm-up before the first real photo.
- photos processed serially.
- 1100 ms cleanup gap after every photo.
- 3500 ms cooldown before a user-triggered full-batch rerun.
- per photo: primary OCR -> sparse-text recovery only if incomplete -> cropped/high-contrast recovery only if still incomplete.
- stop after the bounded local recovery passes; do not hammer the device indefinitely.
- each photo worker is terminated after that photo.

Parser/merge correction:
- CTN lines are tokenized instead of requiring the entire compacted OCR line to be exactly seven characters.
- RT description lines containing `RT_`/multiple underscores are excluded from CTN extraction.
- supplemental passes no longer get discarded wholesale just because candidate extras exceed the numeric gap.
- candidates are voted by logical RT/status/plant ownership; conflicts remain reviewable rather than being silently forced into another group.

Acceptance target remains the field-owner requirement: 10 groups x 5 runs = 50 runs, with at most two groups scoring 4/5 and all others 5/5 (minimum 48/50 overall), with no false PASS / invented CTN.
